**Result**: BESS systems are gaining a lot of traction in the industry. Solar engineers are asked to create and evaluate
projects prospectively and for construction. Having tools that allows them to do these tasks easily will be helpful and
valuable to them.

**Involvement**: I was the technical owner of the feature. I manged stakeholders
and their requirements and distributed work among developers and QA. I've also implemented a large part of the feature.

Feature included:
* BESS placement
* Single line diagram expansion
* Labeling and device overview
* Bill of material expansion
* Yield export
* Copy/Paste support
* UI changes
