date:   2023-11-14 16:11:09 +0300

---

**Result**: We were falling behind our competition in optimal frame generation. There are countless ways how a terrain
following tracker row could be created, only a small subset of them are good enough. After some prototyping I discovered
that we could use the Monte Carlo Tree Search algorithm to generate the frames. This allowed us to catch up and support
terrain following tracker frames.

**Involvement**: I did the research and discovered this approach. It was later implemented by a different developer.
